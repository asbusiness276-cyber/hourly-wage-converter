Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 2026

1. Introduction

At HourlyWageConverter ("we", "our", or "us"), we operate a free online financial wage utility designed to help professionals, employers, and contractors convert salary benchmarks. This Privacy Policy details our commitment to data transparency, our zero-data retention model for calculator inputs, and our cookie and advertising disclosures.

2. Zero Personal Data Collection

Our wage converter is an entirely client-side web application. We do not collect, capture, store, or sell any personal financial data or salary numbers you enter into the calculator. All calculations occur natively inside your browser session and are wiped immediately upon closing or refreshing the page.

3. Standard Log Files

Like virtually all web properties, our hosting infrastructure may record standard automated server logs, which include your device's IP address, browser user-agent, operating system, timestamp, and referring URL. This diagnostic information is strictly used for platform uptime monitoring, DDoS mitigation, and system health.

4. Cookies and Web Storage

We use essential cookies to maintain page responsiveness, load times, and technical functionality. Cookies are small data files placed on your browser. You can control or reject cookies via your browser settings; however, certain web features may perform with reduced efficiency.

5. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdSense)

To fund ongoing infrastructure and keep our financial calculators 100% free, we display third-party advertisements via Google AdSense.

Google, as a third-party vendor, uses cookies (including the DoubleClick cookie) to serve ads based on users' visits to our site and other destinations across the web:

  • Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet.
  • You may opt out of personalized advertising anytime by visiting Google Ads Settings.
  • Alternatively, you can opt out of third-party vendor cookies for personalized ads via AboutAds.info.

6. Children's Privacy

Our services are intended for general audience adults and job-seekers. We do not knowingly harvest or solicit any information from individuals under 13 years of age in accordance with COPPA.

7. Policy Updates & Contact

We may update this policy periodically to reflect evolving regulatory or technological practices. For inquiries regarding this Privacy Policy, reach out to our team at support@hourlywageconverter.com or via our Contact page.
