How to Calculate Your Hourly Wage

Understanding exactly how much you make per hour is crucial. Our Hourly Wage Converter breaks down your annual salary into monthly, weekly, and hourly rates.

The Standard Formula (2,080 Hours)

In the US, a standard full-time employee works 40 hours per week for 52 weeks, totaling 2,080 hours.

Formula: Annual Salary ÷ 2,080 = Hourly Wage

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between Weekly and Bi-Weekly pay?

Weekly pay means 52 paychecks a year. Bi-weekly pay means getting paid every two weeks (26 paychecks a year).

How many working hours are in a year?

In the US, a standard full-time work year is 2,080 hours (40 hours x 52 weeks).
