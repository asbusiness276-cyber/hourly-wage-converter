Calculations & Formulas

How do you calculate hourly wage from an annual salary?

To determine your exact hourly wage from a yearly salary, divide your total annual compensation by the total number of working hours in the year. In the United States, a standard full-time work schedule is 40 hours per week across 52 weeks:

Hourly Wage = Annual Salary ÷ 2,080 Hours (40 hours/week × 52 weeks)

For example, if your annual compensation is $50,000:
$50,000 ÷ 2,080 = $24.04 per hour.

Frequently Asked Questions

How many working hours are in a year?

In the US, a standard full-time work year is 2,080 hours (40 hours × 52 weeks). This accounts for 5 working days per week at 8 hours per day across all 52 weeks of the calendar year.

What is the difference between Weekly and Bi-Weekly pay?

Weekly pay implies 52 paychecks per calendar year. Bi-weekly pay means receiving compensation every two weeks, totaling 26 paychecks per year.

Are calculations pre-tax or post-tax?

All figures represent gross pay prior to statutory federal, state, and local taxes, FICA (Social Security & Medicare), and optional deductions (401k, health insurance).

What about paid time off (PTO) and holidays?

For salaried positions, paid holidays and vacation days are included inside the 2,080 standard hours, so your baseline hourly equivalent remains unaffected.
