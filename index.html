How do you calculate hourly wage from an annual salary?

To calculate your hourly wage from an annual salary, you divide your total yearly earnings by the number of hours you work each year. In the United States, a standard full-time work schedule is 40 hours per week across 52 weeks:

Hourly Wage = Annual Salary ÷ 2,080 Hours (40 hours/week × 52 weeks)

For example, if you earn an annual salary of $50,000, the math is: $50,000 ÷ 2,080 = $24.04 per hour.

How many working hours are in a year?

In the US, a standard full-time work year has exactly 2,080 hours. This calculation accounts for 40 hours per week over all 52 weeks of the calendar year (8 hours/day × 5 days/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours). If you receive paid time off (PTO) or paid holidays, those hours are typically included within this 2,080-hour total.

Gross Pay vs. Take-Home Pay

The calculations generated by this tool represent gross pay, which is your income before any deductions. Your actual net (take-home) paycheck will be lower after accounting for federal, state, and local income taxes, FICA withholdings (Social Security & Medicare), and employer deductions such as health insurance premiums and 401(k) contributions.
