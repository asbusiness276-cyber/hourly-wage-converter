How do you calculate hourly wage from an annual salary?

To determine your hourly wage based on a yearly salary, you need to know how many hours you work per year. In the United States, a standard full-time job is considered 40 hours per week. Assuming you work 52 weeks a year, the total number of working hours is calculated as: 40 hours/week × 52 weeks = 2,080 hours/year.

Simply divide your annual salary by 2,080. For example, if you make $50,000 a year, your hourly wage is $50,000 ÷ 2,080 = $24.04.

How many working hours are in a year?

In the US, a standard full-time work year is 2,080 hours (40 hours × 52 weeks). This accounts for 5 full 8-hour work days per week across all 52 weeks of the calendar year.
